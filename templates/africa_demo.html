A. Variety criteria · exploratory

Choose a planting month. Temperature uses the catalogue’s upper cycle duration and starts on day 1 of that month; it includes complete monthly normals for any months touched. Rainfall is checked annually because the catalogue range is annual.

Summary counts temperature over the crop cycle, annual rainfall and SoilGrids pH (0–30 cm), without weights. The tabs show each criterion separately. Individual totals use available data for that criterion; Summary requires all three. Missing data are not evaluable, never failures.

Data & scientific limitations

Climate tiles were generated for 1991–2020 and passed file integrity checks. A separate offline audit found five raster cells per layer not accounted for by its polygon coverage check; this is under review. It also flagged some unusually high rainfall values for human review. Units and independent source recomputation were not completed by that offline audit.

The map calculates one native pixel per 16 × 16 block (about 15 km at the equator); the reference point is sampled at native resolution. Variety tolerances and observation counts do not enter the score. This is not a crop suitability model or yield prediction.
